Store and label audio with the extensions and MIME types players expect: MP3, WAV, FLAC, Ogg and M4A save as `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.flac`, `.ogg` and `.m4a` with `audio/mpeg`, `audio/wav`, `audio/flac`, `audio/ogg` and `audio/mp4`. Ogg Opus no longer saves as `.opus`, and M4A with an `isom` brand no longer saves as `.mp4` or labels itself `video/mp4`. Adds `getAudioFormatFromUint8Data`.
